1. What the app is

Supagent Social Publisher ("the app") is a private software tool made by Mohamed Amine SLIMANE, an individual developer. It runs on his own computer and is used by him alone, to publish his own finished videos to his own channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and to read those channels' statistics.

The app is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else. It has no public sign-up and connects only to accounts its owner controls.

The app follows the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Anyone who uses the app also agrees to the terms of the platforms it publishes to:

4. Content

  • Only content the owner made or has the rights to is published.
  • Videos made with AI are labelled through each platform's own setting (YouTube containsSyntheticMedia, Instagram's AI-generated label, TikTok's AI-generated content flag) and in the post's caption.
  • Every post follows the platform's community guidelines and API policies.

5. Privacy

How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: it accesses only the owner's own channels, keeps everything on his own computer, and never shares or sells any data.
